Mixpost Lite (formerly Postiz) is the chosen social media scheduling tool.
It's free, self-hosted (Docker or PHP), and supports all needed platforms:
TikTok, Instagram, X, YouTube, Threads, Facebook, LinkedIn. The user no
longer has paid Postiz or Blotato subscriptions.

**Why:** Paid subscriptions were cancelled. Mixpost Lite is the free
self-hosted alternative.

**How to apply:** Set up on a VPS or cloud server with Docker. Needs a
public domain pointing to it.
