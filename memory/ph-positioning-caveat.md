The user explicitly said not to mention "PH-first" or "Philippines-first"
in public-facing social media bios because the PH audience is low-paying.
The product is still PH-first in engineering and product decisions, but
this should not be public positioning.

**Why:** User feedback during social media setup. They want to avoid
signaling to a low-revenue market in public profiles.

**How to apply:** Keep PH-first in internal docs and strategy. Drop it
from social bios, taglines, and public-facing copy.
